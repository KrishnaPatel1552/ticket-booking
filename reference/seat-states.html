Seat states

A seat at one event is free, held, blocked, or a ticket. Payment status is not a fifth state.

  • Freecan be held or blocked
  • Heldone customer, one clock
  • Blockedadmin, with a reason
  • Ticketdoes not free here

While a payment is open

Pending, unknown, and succeeded-without-a-ticket are payment statuses. The seat stays held until lookup returns success or decline.

Allowed moves

  1. Free becomes held when the caps allow it.
  2. Held becomes free when the clock runs out and no payment row exists, when a reschedule ends a bare hold, or when the payment finishes declined.
  3. Checkout on a live hold inserts the payment row and leaves the seat held. If any seat is no longer that customer's live hold, the submit fails and writes no payment row.
  4. Succeeded, including a lookup that returns success, moves held to a ticket.
  5. Unknown leaves the seat held until that lookup.
  6. An admin blocks a free seat, and releases a block back to free.
  7. A hold and a block on the same seat do not change it. The request that locks the seat first stays. The other fails.
  8. A ticket does not become free in this machine.

Sentence to memorize: “A seat at an event is free, held, blocked, or a ticket. If a payment row is pending or unknown, the seat is still held.”
