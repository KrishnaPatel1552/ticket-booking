Five defenses

The five topics Sachin asked you to defend. One sentence each. Practice comes from lesson 3.

1. Charged, but no ticket

I save the payment before I charge. If the charge works and the ticket is missing, the seat stays theirs until a later check writes the ticket. I do not charge a second time.

2. Hold ends during payment

The timer only frees a hold that has no payment. Once checkout has saved a payment, the seat stays with them until we know the result.

3. Six and twelve together

Six on one order. Twelve for that customer at that event. Same customer: I lock first, then count. Same seat, two people: the database keeps one. Too many seats in one request saves nothing.

4. Two machines

A seat is free, held, blocked, or a ticket. A refund is pending, unknown, succeeded, or failed. Unknown does not finish either one. A lookup does.

5. Partial refund

I refund the numbers already stored on that ticket. At 168 hours or more: full net. Through exactly 48 hours: half the net, odd cents round up. Under 48: nothing. The window is real hours, so daylight saving does not stretch it. A customer cancel does not return the fee.
