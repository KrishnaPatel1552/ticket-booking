# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A customer signs in to hold seats at a live event, check out, and cancel tickets. They are at the customer site, choosing a seat and completing an order. An admin does not use this site.

## Product Purpose

Ticket Booking sells seats at live events. This surface is the customer site: sign in, find an event, hold seats, join a waitlist, attach a promo code, check out, and cancel. Success is a customer who can do those actions and tell, at a glance, whether a seat is free, held, blocked, or a ticket.

Harbor Hall is the starter venue. Ticket Booking is the product name.

## Brand Personality

Printed, calm, specific. The site should feel like a program for a particular room: paper and ink, enough quiet to choose a seat. The voice uses the domain words already in `CONTEXT.md`.

## Anti-references

- Ticket marketplace clutter: dense upsells, fee fog, anxious countdowns.
- SaaS dashboards: sidebars, metric cards, generic app chrome.
- Cinematic landing pages: full-bleed heroes, marquees, pinned scroll chapters.

## Design Principles

- The seat map is the event. On the event page, choosing a seat is the primary task.
- Show one next action. Hold, checkout, and cancel do not compete as a single stack.
- Speak the domain words. Hold, order, checkout, ticket, waitlist entry, cancel, refund, promo code, early-bird, price tier, and service fee keep their meanings from `CONTEXT.md`.
- Stay calm. The customer is picking a seat, without urgency theater.
- A seat's state is understandable without color and without a pointer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. The seat map works from the keyboard. Reduced motion is honored. Free, held, blocked, and ticket are never communicated by color alone.
