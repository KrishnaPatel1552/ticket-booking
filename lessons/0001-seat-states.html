What state is the seat in?

Sachin said the interview will ask about every allowed move of a booking. The answer starts with four states. Payment results are not extra states.

This is the opening of the Copart SDET interview: you explain the take-home, then he picks a live change. If the first question is “walk me through a seat,” this is the whole answer.

Four states

  • Freenobody has it
  • Heldone customer
  • Blockedan admin
  • Ticketsold

One seat at one event is exactly one of those. The same physical seat can be sold at a different event. A hold and a ticket are one row: becoming a ticket updates that row. It does not create a second one.

Pending, unknown, and a success that has not written the ticket yet are payment statuses. While any of those is open, the seat stays held. The hold clock does not free it once a payment row exists.

The moves he can point at

  1. Free becomes held when the caps allow it.
  2. Held becomes free in three cases only: the clock runs out and there is no payment row, a reschedule ends a bare hold, or the payment finishes as declined.
  3. Checkout inserts the payment row and leaves the seat held.
  4. Success, including a later lookup, makes it a ticket.
  5. Unknown leaves it held until that lookup.
  6. An admin blocks a free seat, or releases a block back to free.
  7. A hold and a block on the same seat: the first lock stays, the other request fails.
  8. A ticket does not become free in this machine. That move belongs to refunds, which is a later lesson.

The compressed card is Seat states. Read this page once, then answer from memory.

Say it, then check

He describes a moment. You name the state.

Each choice is the same length. Pick the state the seat is in after the moment he describes.

No payment row exists. The hold clock has run out. Someone opens the event.

Checkout started. The mock result is unknown. No lookup has returned yet.

The seat is already held. An admin then tries to block that same seat.

A later status lookup returns success for that payment.

The customer never submits checkout. The hold clock is still running.
